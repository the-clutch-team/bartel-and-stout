Instrument cleaning

Remove accumulated dust and residue from microscope optics and working surfaces.

Service overview

When an image looks hazy

Dust, fingerprints, or residue can make a familiar specimen look unfamiliar. Tell the coordinator where the marks appear and whether they move when you change objectives or slides.

What to include in your request

  • A description of the affected optics
  • The objectives or viewing positions involved
  • When the instrument was last cleaned
